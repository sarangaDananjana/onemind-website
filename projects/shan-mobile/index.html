Our Promise

The P1+ Guarantee

P1+ isn't just marketing — it's a strict standard every device must pass before reaching our shelves.

Cosmetic Standard

What P1+ actually means

The cosmetic scale runs P1 through P5. P1+ is our highest tier — reserved for devices that appear near-new under normal light.

  • No cracks, chips, or visible deep scratches
  • Micro-scratches are invisible at arm's length
  • Screen in perfect condition with no dead pixels
  • All buttons, speakers, and ports fully functional
1-Year Hardware Warranty
Included with every device

A full calendar year of hardware coverage — in writing, with a physical warranty card. If a hardware fault develops naturally, we fix it. No debates.

What others won't tell you

Before buying from any seller, ask them directly:

  • Has the battery percentage been reset via software?
  • Are all internal parts original Apple components?
  • Is the IMEI verified clean and fully unlocked?
  • Do they offer a written warranty?
